# Credits

This project stands on the work of several earlier communities and maintainers.

## Primary Sources

- [e-maxx.ru / algo](https://e-maxx.ru/algo), the original Russian algorithm reference created and maintained by the e-maxx / ru.maxx community.
- [cp-algorithms](https://cp-algorithms.com/), which adapted and expanded that material into the English resource used by competitive programmers worldwide.

## Contributors

- The [cp-algorithms contributors](https://github.com/cp-algorithms/cp-algorithms/graphs/contributors), for writing, editing, reviewing, and maintaining the reference articles.
- Volunteer translators and maintainers of algobangla, for making this material accessible in Bangla.

## Acknowledgement

algobangla is an adaptation and translation effort. Credit for the original algorithm articles belongs to the e-maxx / ru.maxx project and the cp-algorithms community.
